E-Commerce Customer Reviews Analysis (SQL)

1. Project Background
An online women's clothing retailer holds over 23,000 customer review records. This project aims to evaluate product category performance, understand customer age demographics, and measure overall customer satisfaction and recommendation rates using SQL.

2. Dataset & Tools
- Dataset: Women's E-Commerce Clothing Reviews (23,486 rows)
- Tools: MySQL (MySQL Workbench)

3. Business Questions & Key Insights
1. Product Category Performance & Popularity:
- The Tops department dominates review volume with 9,427 reviews, followed by Dresses (5,353 reviews) and Bottoms (3,230 reviews).
- Bottoms, Intimate, and Jackets achieved the highest average satisfaction score (4.28), whereas Trend had the lowest performance in both review volume (100 reviews) and average rating (3.85).

2. Customer Age Demographics:
- The core customer base consists of mature adult shoppers, with an average age ranging between 42 and 44.5 years old across all clothing departments.

3. Product Recommendation Rate:
- Customer recommendation rates align closely with average ratings, where top-performing categories maintain recommendation rates above 80%, while the Trend category requires immediate product evaluation.

4. Business Recommendations
- Evaluate the Trend Category: The product team should review fabric quality, sizing consistency, and product descriptions for items in the Trend department to reduce negative feedback and boost recommendation rates.
- Targeted Marketing Strategy: Focus promotional campaigns on the 35-50 age demographic and introduce product bundling between high-volume items (Tops) and highest-rated categories (Bottoms and Jackets).
